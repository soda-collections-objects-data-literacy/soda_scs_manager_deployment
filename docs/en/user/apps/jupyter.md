## Overview

JupyterHub is your personal data science workspace and a powerful, interactive environment where you can analyze data, create visualizations, and conduct research without needing to install any software on your computer.

When you access your JupyterHub environment, you get a complete toolkit for data analysis and research that includes everything you need to get started immediately.

## Contained softwares

- Interactive Notebooks: Work with live documents that combine code, visualizations, and explanatory text all in one place. These notebooks let you experiment with data, test ideas, and document your research process step-by-step. Multiple Programming Languages: Your environment comes ready with:
  - Python - Perfect for data analysis, machine learning, and scientific computing
  - R - Ideal for statistical analysis and advanced data visualization
  - Julia - High-performance computing for complex mathematical operations
- OpenRefine Integration: Clean and transform messy datasets with an intuitive, visual interface. Perfect for preparing data before analysis or fixing inconsistencies in large datasets.

**Note:** To open files from your Nextcloud inside Open Refine using the `Get data from Web Addresses (URLs)` field, you must append `/download` to the public file sharing link. E.g. `https://drive.scs.sammlungen.io/s/[file_sharing_id]/download`.

- Connected File Storage: Your JupyterHub workspace seamlessly connects to your WissKI research environments, making it easy to share files, datasets, and results between different tools in your research workflow.

## Possibilities

**Process cultural heritage data** with specialized tools and libraries.

**Prepare data for publication** by cleaning, validating, and formatting your research data for sharing or integration with other systems like WissKI.

**Explore and Analyze Data** by uploading datasets and immediately start exploring them with interactive charts, statistical summaries, and custom analysis scripts.

**Create compelling visualizations** by generating publication-ready graphs, charts, and interactive visualizations to communicate your findings effectively.
